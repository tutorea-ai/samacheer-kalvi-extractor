PICTOGRAMMAR

A sentence begins with a capital letter and ends with a full stop or a question mark or an exclamation mark.

A sentence can do four things.

● When a sentence is a statement, it gives a fact or an opinion and we end it with a full stop.

Ducks swim in water.

The description of the picture is begins

The picture of three ducks sailing in the water is given.

The description of the picture is ends

● When a sentence asks a question, we end it with a question mark.

What do you want?

The description of the picture is begins

Teacher and student in classroom is shown

The description of the picture is ends

● When a sentence makes a request or gives an instruction or a command, we end it with a full stop. Take this road.

The description of the picture is begins

The picture shows a man standing near a road signal is given.

The description of the picture is ends

● When a sentence expresses surprise, wonder or some strong emotion, we end it with an exclamation mark.

How good these flowers smell!

The description of the picture is begins

A girl smells a bunch of flowers is shown in the picture.

The description of the picture is ends
