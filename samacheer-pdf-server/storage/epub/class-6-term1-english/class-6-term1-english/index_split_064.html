I Dream of Spices

The description of the picture is begins

A mother and her son picture is given. The mother has a basket in her left hand and the right hand is on her son’s shoulder.

The description of the picture is ends

My mother would say:

“Little boy Raj

Go to Muthu’s

and get some

cinnamon, betel leaves

and ginger and garlic.”

The description of the picture is begins

The son is walking with a basket in his right-hand picture is given

The description of the picture is ends

And so, I go to the shops

singing all the way

The description of the picture is begins

The shopkeeper is inside the shop and the boy with the basket stands outside the shop picture is given

The description of the picture is ends

and when Muthu asks me

what I’d want

I rattle off a list:

“Sesame seeds, onions tomatoes and pickles”

The description of the picture is begins

The mother twists the ear of the son picture is given

The description of the picture is ends

And back home,

Mother twists my ears

Ouch!

Author, Raj Arumugam

GLOSSARY

cinnamon - the bark of a tree that gives a delicious flavour to food

garlic - a small bulb with a strong taste used in cooking
